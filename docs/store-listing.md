# Play Store listing

Copy and form answers for the Phase 5 submission. Written here rather than typed
straight into the Play Console so it can be reviewed, corrected and reused — the
console has no history and no diff.

Everything factual below was checked against the code, not remembered. Where a
claim depends on the build rather than the source it says so.

a puzzle game nobody can describe to a friend does not spread.

## Short description

Play limits this to **80 characters**. It is the line that appears under the
icon in search results and it does more work than the full description.

> Route belts, press and assemble. One new factory puzzle a day, scored like golf.

*(79 characters.)*

Golf is the hook: it promises a target to beat rather than a level to finish,
which is the actual shape of the game.

---

## Full description

Play limits this to 4000 characters; this is well under, deliberately. Nobody
reads a wall of text on a store page.

> A new factory puzzle every day.
>
> Items come out of a source and have to reach a sink. Between them you lay
> conveyor belts, presses that transform one item into another, splitters that
> fan a line in two, and assemblers that combine two items into something new.
> Get the factory running and the order delivered.
>
> Every puzzle has a par — the cheapest solution known — and your score is what
> you spent against it. Coming in at par is good. Coming in under it is better,
> and it is possible: par is the cheapest solution the puzzle generator found,
> not a proven minimum.
>
> • One puzzle a day, the same one for everybody
> • Scored like golf, against par
> • Streaks and history, kept on your device
> • A share card that gives your score away and never the answer
> • No accounts, no adverts, no data collection, nothing sent anywhere
>
> The puzzles are generated by a program and then checked by another one, which
> plays each candidate before it is allowed in — so every puzzle you are given is
> known to be solvable, known to fit its budget, and known to have more than one
> good answer.
>
> Plays in a browser too, at nuenoir.github.io/factory-puzzle

The last two paragraphs are the differentiator and worth keeping. "Verified
solvable, verified more than one answer" is a promise most puzzle games cannot
make and this one can, because the validator is the same engine as the game.

## Content rating questionnaire

Play's questionnaire is a series of yes/no questions. Every answer here is
**no**: no violence, no sexuality, no profanity, no controlled substances, no
gambling or simulated gambling, no user-to-user communication, no location
sharing, no personal information collected or shared, no purchases.

Expected outcome: **Everyone / PEGI 3 / rated for all ages**.

If the questionnaire asks whether the app is "primarily for children" the honest
answer is no — it is not aimed at children, it simply has nothing in it that
would matter to one. Answering yes pulls the app into the Families programme and
its extra requirements for no benefit.

## Data safety form

This is the section most likely to be filled in wrongly out of caution, so the
answers are spelled out with the reason.

| Question | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** | No network requests exist in the app at all. `grep` for `fetch`, `XMLHttpRequest`, `WebSocket`, analytics and crash SDKs returns nothing, and the only runtime dependencies are Expo, React and React Native. |
| Is all user data encrypted in transit? | *n/a* | Nothing is transmitted. |
| Do you provide a way for users to request data deletion? | *n/a* | Nothing is collected. Local history is erased by clearing app storage. |

**Puzzle history is not "collected".** Play's definition of collection is data
*transmitted off the device*. The streak record is written to the device's own
storage and never leaves it, which the form treats the same as a save file. It
is described in the privacy policy anyway, because a player deserves to know it
exists even where the form does not ask.

Do **not** tick "Device or other IDs". Nothing reads an advertising ID or any
other identifier.

## Graphics

Not written here, because they are artwork rather than copy. Required:

| Asset | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| App icon | 512×512 PNG, no transparency | The hex grid is the obvious motif |
| Feature graphic | 1024×500 PNG | Shown at the top of the listing |
| Phone screenshots | at least 2, 16:9 or 9:16 | A solved board, and the share card |

Screenshots can come straight from the web build at a phone viewport — the board
is the same board. Take them of a *solved* factory with items on the belts; an
empty grid tells a browsing player nothing.

The icon and feature graphic need a real design pass. A generated placeholder
would be worse than a plain one, and the store will happily reject a screenshot
that is mostly empty dark background.
